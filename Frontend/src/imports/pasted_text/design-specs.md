3. Typography Setup
Use your Gotham Ultra only for headings.
Example scale:
Level Title — 24px Gotham Ultra
Section Title — 18px Gotham Medium
Body — 14px Gotham Book
Caption — 12px Gotham Book
Otherwise Gotham Ultra everywhere will look like someone shouting through the interface.
4. Color Usage
Use red sparingly.
Good usage:
progress
active level
CTA button
Bad usage:
every card
every heading
You want contrast, not a Ferrari exploded on the screen.
